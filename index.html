02 · City guide · See + Do

Montréal essentials.

The city beyond the itinerary: old buildings, museums, markets, viewpoints and neighbourhoods worth keeping in your pocket for a free afternoon.

If you only do five:
Old Montréal · Notre-Dame Basilica · Mont Royal · Plateau & Mile End · Jean-Talon Market.
★ Essential · 2–3 hours

Old Montréal

Cobbled streets, historic façades and the city's most immediately photogenic quarter. Wander Rue Saint-Paul, Place Jacques-Cartier and the Old Port rather than treating it as a checklist.

Best for · architecture · wandering · photographs
Google Maps
★ Essential · 45–60 mins

Notre-Dame Basilica

Gothic Revival drama, deep blue interiors, gilding and stained glass. Even if churches are not normally the point of a city break, this one earns the stop.

Best for · architecture · history · rainy day
Google Maps
★ Essential · 1–2 hours

Mont Royal

Head for Belvédère Kondiaronk for the classic skyline view, then wander through the park without feeling obliged to turn it into a hike.

Best for · city view · sunset · photographs
Viewpoint
Neighbourhood · half day

Plateau Mont-Royal

Colourful exterior staircases, handsome terraces, independent shops, cafés and the sort of streets that make Montréal feel lived-in rather than staged for visitors.

Best for · cute buildings · coffee · street photography
Google Maps
Neighbourhood · 2–3 hours

Mile End

A creative, slightly scruffier Montréal of coffee, bagels, murals and small shops. Better approached as somewhere to wander than somewhere to sightsee.

Best paired with · Café Olimpico and an unhurried day
Google Maps
Food · 1–2 hours

Jean-Talon Market

A classic Montréal market stop for Québec produce, cheese, bakeries and prepared food. Ideal for a late morning that gradually becomes lunch.

Best for · local flavour · casual lunch · people-watching

Museum · 2 hours

Montréal Museum of Fine Arts

The heavyweight art option, with Canadian and international collections and enough breadth to make it the one museum to choose if you only want one.

Best for · art · design · wet afternoons
Google Maps
History · 90 mins

Pointe-à-Callière

Archaeology and Montréal history built around the place where the city began. A strong choice when you want context as well as something to look at.

Best for · history · Old Montréal · rainy day
Google Maps
Architecture · quick detour

Habitat 67

Moshe Safdie's extraordinary Expo 67 housing complex: brutalist, strange and instantly recognisable. A very different Montréal photograph.

Best for · architecture · design · Instagram
Google Maps
Outdoors · 1–3 hours

Lachine Canal

Waterside Montréal without leaving the city. Walk a section rather than conquering the whole canal; it's ideal when you want air rather than another formal attraction.

Best for · walking · cycling · a slower afternoon
Google Maps
Garden · 2–3 hours

Montréal Botanical Garden

A major green-space attraction with themed gardens and glasshouses. Choose it when you want a quiet contrast to downtown.

Best for · greenery · photography · a slower day
Google Maps
Local Montréal · 1–2 hours

Marché Atwater

Smaller and handsome beside the Lachine Canal. A good alternative to Jean-Talon when your day is taking you south-west rather than towards Little Italy.

Best for · food · architecture · canal wandering
Google Maps
The Richie & Alex version:
Don't attempt all twelve. Old Montréal + Notre-Dame give you the historic city; Mont Royal gives you the photograph; Plateau + Mile End give you the city people actually live in. Everything else is there for weather, mood and spare time.
03 · Sunday

The road trip.

A full day without making it an endurance event. The aim is Laurentian scenery, lakes and photographs — not ticking off towns.

30 August · Laurentians

Montréal → Mont-Tremblant

Fairmont → Saint-Sauveur → Mont-Tremblant → Lac Mercier → Montréal

Leave Montréal early enough to enjoy the drive rather than chase the clock. Stop in Saint-Sauveur for coffee, then continue north into the Laurentians. Mont-Tremblant gives you the colourful village; Lac Mercier gives you the quieter Canada moment.

Keep the return flexible and aim to be back in Montréal before the evening becomes a rush.
